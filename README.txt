This is supposed to become a little RPG made in unity. Im not an artist so its probably gonna look really bad until i tend to that kind of stuff.

In the game the character in this game is summoned through a portal without memory of having existed before. Some creepy cultists tell them theire being controlled by a god who wants to see the world through their eyes. They then tell them to get out there and show this god the world, get stronger and perform miracles. 

Right now you can do the following things: Clicking on Air: Attack (if you have equipped a weapon on the left space); click on chest: open it -> item drops; click on item -> collect it; press R -> open inventory -> click on one of the four different equipment spaces (the bottom one is for boots (there are only boots and a sword right now)) -> select an item by clicking on it -> if there isnt anything in this section yet press escape to return. press R again to close the inventory. Also there is an NPC you can talk to by clicking on him; you can speed up his dialogue by pressing space; press space when he has finished talking to go on; click on a dialogue option to choose it; do not press space if dialogue options are being displayed.If you dismiss his prayer he will walk away.

The base idea is that you can just do anything you want in this game, open up a shop, help people or slay monsters. I probably never gonna be able to finish this or get somewhere near to where i want to get, but i hope the journey to the inevitable point of giving up will be fun.
